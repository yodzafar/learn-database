# ClickHouse rejasi: real-time analitik (OLAP) baza

Nima uchun alohida: ClickHouse milliardlab qatorli jadval ustida aggregatsiyani soniyaning bir qismida bajaradigan ochiq kodli columnar OLAP baza. Product analytics, observability (log, metrika, trace), ad-tech, moliya tahlili va real-time dashboard'lar uchun sanoat standartlaridan biri (Cloudflare, Uber, eBay, Yandex Metrica). U OLTP bazalardan butunlay boshqacha fikrlashni talab qiladi: `UPDATE` va `DELETE` qimmat, `JOIN` cheklangan, primary key unique emas, ma'lumot katta batch'larda yoziladi va fonda "merge" qilinadi. Bu modul shu fikrlash modelini va MergeTree oilasini chuqur o'rgatadi. Umumiy warehouse va ETL nazariyasi (dimensional modeling, ELT, dbt) `data-engineering/` modulida, bu yerda ClickHouse implementatsiyasi.

Boshlash: `theory/` dagi storage bosqichi (row vs columnar, LSM-tree, compression) va `sql/` I–IV bosqich (aggregatsiya, window function, CTE). `postgres/` I–II bosqich tanish bo'lsa, OLTP va OLAP farqi aniqroq seziladi. `data-engineering/` dagi fayl formatlari (Parquet) mavzusi parallel o'tilsa yaxshi.

Ishlash tartibi: `clickhouse/docs/NN-nom.md` nazariya va vazifalar, kod `clickhouse/NN-slug/main.sql` (yoki `task_N.sql`) va `test.sql` (natijani kutilgan qiymat bilan solishtiruvchi so'rovlar, `throwIf` bilan). Muhit: `make up db=clickhouse` (`clickhouse/clickhouse-server` LTS image, konteyner `ldb-clickhouse`, HTTP 8123, native 9000), `make shell db=clickhouse` (`clickhouse-client`), `make ch n=03` (`clickhouse/03-*/main.sql` ni `clickhouse-client --multiquery` bilan ishga tushiradi), `make test d=clickhouse/03-...`, `make reset db=clickhouse`, `make check` (sqlfluff `clickhouse` dialekti). Katta dataset'lar (ClickHouse rasmiy example dataset'lari: NYC Taxi, UK Property Prices, GitHub Events, OnTime) `clickhouse/data/` ga yuklanadi va git'ga kirmaydi; Mac'da diskni hisobga olib kichik qismlari olinadi. Replikatsiya mavzularida `make up db=clickhouse-cluster` (2 shard x 2 replika + 3 ClickHouse Keeper, kichik).

## I bosqich: Arxitektura va asoslar

1. **OLAP va columnar arxitektura**: OLTP vs OLAP yuklama (qisqa nuqtali so'rovlar vs keng skan va aggregatsiya), row-oriented vs column-oriented saqlash (faqat kerakli ustunni o'qish, bir tipli ma'lumotning yaxshi siqilishi), vectorized query execution (blok bo'yicha ishlov, SIMD), ClickHouse tarixi (Yandex Metrica), shared-nothing arxitektura, part'lar va fon merge'lar (LSM-tree g'oyasiga o'xshashlik), ClickHouse nima uchun tez va nimada sekin (nuqtali update, ko'p kichik insert, katta JOIN, yuqori concurrency kichik so'rovlar), DuckDB (in-process), Druid/Pinot (real-time OLAP), Snowflake/BigQuery (managed DW) bilan qisqa solishtirish
2. **O'rnatish, klient va birinchi qadamlar**: docker'da ClickHouse server, `clickhouse-client` (interaktiv, `--multiquery`, `--format`, `--query`, `--param_x` parametrlar), HTTP interfeys (`curl 'http://localhost:8123/?query=...'`, `FORMAT JSONEachRow`), `clickhouse-local` (serversiz, fayl ustidan SQL), Play UI, kirish formatlari (`CSV`, `TSV`, `JSONEachRow`, `Parquet`, `Native`, `RowBinary`) va chiqish formatlari (`Pretty`, `Vertical`), `INSERT ... FORMAT`, `file()`, `url()`, `s3()` table function'lar bilan fayldan to'g'ridan-to'g'ri so'rov, example dataset yuklash, database va table yaratish, `system` database (`system.tables`, `system.columns`, `system.parts`, `system.settings`), settings ierarxiyasi (server config, profile, session `SET`, query `SETTINGS`)
3. **Ma'lumot tiplari**: butun sonlar (`UInt8..UInt256`, `Int8..Int256`) va to'g'ri o'lcham tanlash nega muhim, `Float32/64` va `Decimal(P,S)`, `String` (baytlar, uzunlik cheklovsiz) va `FixedString(N)`, `LowCardinality(String)` (dictionary encoding, qachon foyda, qachon zarar: taxminan 10 mingdan kam noyob qiymat), `Nullable(T)` va uning narxi (alohida null-mask fayl, indekslashga to'sqinlik, default qiymat afzal), `Date`/`Date32`/`DateTime`/`DateTime64(precision, 'timezone')` va timezone tuzoqlari, `UUID`, `IPv4`/`IPv6`, `Enum8/16`, `Bool`, kompozit tiplar: `Array(T)`, `Tuple(...)`, `Map(K, V)`, `Nested`, yangi `JSON` tip va `Dynamic`/`Variant` (versiyani hujjatdan tekshirish), geo tiplar qisqa, `toTypeName`, implicit conversion yo'qligi va `CAST`/`toInt32OrZero`/`toDateOrNull` oilalari
4. **Compression va codec'lar**: ustun bo'yicha siqish, default `LZ4` va `ZSTD(level)`, maxsus codec'lar: `Delta`, `DoubleDelta` (monoton timestamp), `Gorilla` (float vaqt qatori), `T64`, `FPC`, codec zanjiri (`CODEC(Delta, ZSTD(3))`), `system.columns` da `data_compressed_bytes` va `data_uncompressed_bytes` bilan siqish nisbatini o'lchash, tip tanlash + `ORDER BY` + codec birgalikda siqishni qanday hal qilishi (ma'lumotni saralash eng katta ta'sir), vazifa: bir xil dataset'ni turli sxema bilan saqlab o'lchamini solishtirish

## II bosqich: MergeTree chuqur

5. **MergeTree: part, granula va sparse primary index**: `ENGINE = MergeTree ORDER BY (...)` va `PRIMARY KEY` farqi (primary key `ORDER BY` prefiksi bo'lishi mumkin), har `INSERT` yangi part yaratishi, part tuzilishi (`columns.txt`, `.bin` va `.mrk` fayllar, `primary.idx`), wide vs compact part, granula (`index_granularity = 8192` qator) va adaptive granularity, sparse index nima va nega B-tree emas (har granula uchun bitta yozuv, xotiraga sig'adi), so'rov qanday granulalarni tashlab ketadi (`EXPLAIN indexes = 1`), primary key unique emasligi, fon merge'lar (`system.merges`, `system.part_log`), `OPTIMIZE TABLE ... FINAL` va nega uni production'da muntazam chaqirmaslik kerak, "too many parts" xatosi va sababi
6. **ORDER BY va primary key dizayni**: kalit ustunlarini tanlash qoidalari (filtrlarda tez-tez ishlatiladigan, past kardinallikdan yuqoriga), kardinallik tartibi siqish va skanga qanday ta'sir qiladi, vaqt ustunini kalitda qayerga qo'yish (`toStartOfHour(ts)` kabi qisqartirish), bir nechta so'rov patterni uchun bitta jadval yetmasa nima qilish (projection, MV, ikkinchi jadval), `EXPLAIN indexes = 1` va `system.query_log` dagi `read_rows`/`read_bytes` bilan kalit sifatini o'lchash, noto'g'ri kalit tanlash oqibatlari laboratoriyasi (bir dataset, uch xil `ORDER BY`, natija jadvali)
7. **Partitioning, TTL va data skipping indekslar**: `PARTITION BY toYYYYMM(date)` (partition boshqaruv uchun, tezlik uchun emas: ko'p partition zarar), partition pruning, partition amallari (`DROP PARTITION`, `DETACH`/`ATTACH`, `MOVE PARTITION`, `REPLACE PARTITION`, `FREEZE` backup uchun), TTL (qator TTL: `TTL date + INTERVAL 30 DAY DELETE`, ustun TTL, `TO VOLUME`/`TO DISK` bilan hot/cold storage, `GROUP BY` bilan rollup TTL, `ttl_only_drop_parts`), data skipping indekslar (`INDEX idx col TYPE minmax/set(N)/bloom_filter/ngrambf_v1/tokenbf_v1 GRANULARITY n`), skip index qachon yordam beradi va qachon foydasiz (ma'lumot `ORDER BY` bilan korrelatsiya qilinmasa), `MATERIALIZE INDEX`, full-text index holati (versiyani tekshirish)
8. **MergeTree oilasi: Replacing, Summing, Aggregating**: `ReplacingMergeTree(ver[, is_deleted])` (dedup merge vaqtida, kafolat yo'q, `FINAL` modifikatori va uning narxi, `argMax` bilan dedup so'rov patterni, upsert emulyatsiyasi), `SummingMergeTree` (bir xil kalitli qatorlarni yig'ish, `sum` bilan so'rash shartligi), `AggregatingMergeTree` va `AggregateFunction(uniq, UInt64)`/`SimpleAggregateFunction(max, ...)` ustunlari, `-State` bilan yozish va `-Merge` bilan o'qish, nega "merge bo'ladi" degan taxmin bilan so'rov yozish xato (doim aggregatsiya bilan so'rash), qaysi engine qachon (jadval)
9. **CollapsingMergeTree, VersionedCollapsingMergeTree va o'zgaruvchan ma'lumot**: holatni o'zgartirish muammosi (foydalanuvchi sessiyasi, buyurtma statusi), `CollapsingMergeTree(sign)`: `+1`/`-1` qatorlar bilan "bekor qilish" va qayta yozish, tartib talabi va buzilish holatlari, `VersionedCollapsingMergeTree(sign, version)` tartibsiz kelgan ma'lumot uchun, so'rov patterni (`sum(sign * value)`, `HAVING sum(sign) > 0`), CDC oqimini ClickHouse'ga yozishda qaysi engine (ReplacingMergeTree `is_deleted` bilan vs Collapsing), boshqa engine'lar qisqa: `Log`/`TinyLog`/`StripeLog`, `Memory`, `Buffer`, `Null` (MV uchun), `Join`, `Set`, `GraphiteMergeTree`

## III bosqich: So'rov va aggregatsiya

10. **ClickHouse SQL o'ziga xosliklari**: `SELECT` kengaytmalari: `LIMIT n BY col` (har guruhdan n tadan), `ARRAY JOIN`, `WITH` (ifoda alias va subquery), `PREWHERE` (avval yengil ustun bilan filtrlash, avtomatik optimizatsiya), `SAMPLE` (`SAMPLE BY` kalit bilan approximate so'rov), `FINAL`, `SETTINGS` so'rov ichida, `GROUP BY ... WITH ROLLUP/CUBE/TOTALS`, `ORDER BY ... WITH FILL` (vaqt qatoridagi bo'shliqlarni to'ldirish), `QUALIFY`, window functions (qo'llab-quvvatlanadigan frame'lar), lambda funksiyalar va higher-order array funksiyalar (`arrayMap`, `arrayFilter`, `arrayReduce`, `arraySort`, `arrayJoin`, `groupArray`), `if`/`multiIf`, string va regex funksiyalar (`extract`, `match`, `splitByChar`), sana funksiyalar (`toStartOfInterval`, `dateDiff`, `toTimeZone`), query parameters (`{name:Type}`) va SQL injection'dan himoya
11. **Aggregate funksiyalar va kombinatorlar**: asosiylar va o'ziga xoslar (`any`, `anyLast`, `argMin`/`argMax`, `groupArray`, `groupUniqArray`, `topK`, `sumMap`, `histogram`), kombinatorlar: `-If` (`countIf`, `sumIf`), `-Array`, `-Distinct`, `-OrDefault`/`-OrNull`, `-Resample`, `-ForEach`, `-State`/`-Merge`/`-MergeState` (oraliq holatni saqlash va birlashtirish: incremental aggregatsiyaning asosi), `finalizeAggregation`, `runningAccumulate`, funnel va retention funksiyalari (`windowFunnel`, `retention`, `sequenceMatch`/`sequenceCount`), product analytics so'rovlari (DAU/MAU, funnel konversiyasi, cohort retention)
12. **Approximate hisoblash**: aniq vs taxminiy aggregatsiya trade-off (xotira va tezlik vs xato), `uniq` (adaptive sampling), `uniqCombined` (HyperLogLog asosida), `uniqHLL12`, `uniqExact` va uning xotira narxi, `quantile` (reservoir sampling), `quantileTDigest`, `quantileTiming`, `quantileExact`, `quantiles(0.5, 0.9, 0.99)`, `SAMPLE 0.1` bilan so'rov, HyperLogLog va t-digest algoritmlari g'oyasi, xato chegarasini o'lchash vazifasi (aniq va taxminiy natijani solishtirib), p99 latency dashboard uchun qaysi funksiya
13. **JOIN, dictionaries va ularning cheklovlari**: JOIN turlari (`INNER`, `LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `FULL`, `CROSS`, `ANY`, `ALL`, `SEMI`, `ANTI`, `ASOF JOIN` vaqt qatorlari uchun), default hash join (o'ng jadval xotiraga yuklanadi: kichik jadval o'ngda qoidasi), join algoritmlari (`join_algorithm`: `hash`, `parallel_hash`, `grace_hash`, `partial_merge`, `full_sorting_merge`, `direct`), `max_bytes_in_join`, JOIN o'rniga denormalizatsiya qachon afzal, dictionaries (`CREATE DICTIONARY`: manba ClickHouse/PostgreSQL/MySQL/fayl/HTTP, layout `flat`/`hashed`/`range_hashed`/`cache`/`ip_trie`, `LIFETIME`, `dictGet`, `dictHas`), dictionary bilan dimension lookup, `Join` engine, distributed JOIN (`GLOBAL JOIN`, `GLOBAL IN`) ga ko'prik

## IV bosqich: Ma'lumot oqimi va integratsiya

14. **Insert strategiyasi va ma'lumot yuklash**: nega har insert part yaratadi va kichik insert'lar zararli (qoida: sekundiga bittadan kam, har biri ming qatordan ko'p), batch'lash ilova tomonida, `async_insert = 1` va `wait_for_async_insert` (server tomonida buferlash, kafolatlar), `Buffer` engine eski usul, insert deduplication (`insert_deduplicate`, block hash, idempotent retry), katta fayllarni yuklash (`INSERT INTO ... SELECT FROM s3(...)`, `clickhouse-client` bilan `Parquet`), parallel yuklash, ilovadan yozish: Go (`clickhouse-go` v2, native protokol, batch API), Python (`clickhouse-connect`), `app/` moduli bilan bog'lash
15. **Materialized views va refreshable MV**: incremental MV: insert trigger sifatida (manba jadvalga kelgan har blok `SELECT` dan o'tib target jadvalga yoziladi), `TO target` bilan aniq target jadval (`POPULATE` dan qochish va nega), MV + `AggregatingMergeTree`/`SummingMergeTree` bilan real-time rollup (minutlik, soatlik aggregatlar), MV zanjirlari, MV tuzoqlari (faqat yangi insert'larni ko'radi, JOIN o'ng jadval o'zgarishini ko'rmaydi, MV xatosi insert'ni buzadi), backfill qilish usuli, refreshable materialized view (`REFRESH EVERY 1 HOUR`, to'liq qayta hisoblash, `APPEND`, dependency: versiyani hujjatdan tekshirish), projections (`ADD PROJECTION ... SELECT ... ORDER BY`/`GROUP BY`, `MATERIALIZE PROJECTION`, avtomatik tanlanishi, MV bilan farqi)
16. **Mutations, UPDATE va DELETE**: `ALTER TABLE ... UPDATE/DELETE` mutation sifatida (asinxron, butun part'larni qayta yozadi, `system.mutations`, `mutations_sync`, `KILL MUTATION`), lightweight `DELETE FROM` (`_row_exists` maska, keyingi merge'da tozalanadi), lightweight update holati (versiyani hujjatdan tekshirish), `ALTER TABLE ... DELETE IN PARTITION`, GDPR o'chirish talabi va uni ClickHouse'da qanday bajarish, o'zgaruvchan ma'lumot uchun dizayn alternativlari (ReplacingMergeTree, partition almashtirish, `REPLACE PARTITION`), sxema o'zgarishi (`ALTER TABLE ADD/DROP/MODIFY COLUMN`, metadata-only amallar va qimmat amallar)
17. **Integratsiya table engine'lari va table function'lar**: `Kafka` engine (topic'dan iste'mol: `kafka_broker_list`, `kafka_topic_list`, `kafka_group_name`, `kafka_format`; Kafka jadvali + MV + MergeTree klassik pattern, offset commit va at-least-once, xatoli xabarlar `kafka_handle_error_mode`), `S3`/`S3Queue` engine va `s3()` function (Parquet data lake ustidan so'rov, `minio` bilan lokal), `PostgreSQL`/`MySQL` engine va `MaterializedPostgreSQL` (experimental) CDC, `postgresql()`/`mysql()` table function bilan federated so'rov, `URL`, `File`, `HDFS` qisqa, `Iceberg`/`DeltaLake` table function'lar (lakehouse'ni o'qish), Debezium/ClickPipes qisqa, `data-engineering/` dagi Kafka va CDC mavzulariga ko'prik

## V bosqich: Klaster va ekspluatatsiya

18. **Replikatsiya va ClickHouse Keeper**: `ReplicatedMergeTree('/clickhouse/tables/{shard}/table', '{replica}')`, macros, ClickHouse Keeper (ZooKeeper o'rnini bosuvchi, Raft asosida) va uning roli (part'lar ro'yxati, replication log, insert dedup), multi-master yozish (har replikaga yozish mumkin), replikatsiya kechikishi (`system.replicas`, `system.replication_queue`), `ON CLUSTER` DDL, replika yo'qolganda tiklash (`SYSTEM RESTORE REPLICA`), `SYSTEM SYNC REPLICA`, Keeper'ning eng ko'p uchraydigan muammolari, `Replicated` database engine
19. **Sharding va Distributed table**: `Distributed(cluster, db, table, sharding_key)` engine (o'zi ma'lumot saqlamaydi, so'rovni shard'larga yuboradi va natijalarni birlashtiradi), `remote_servers` konfiguratsiyasi, sharding key tanlash (`rand()` vs `cityHash64(user_id)` lokallik uchun), Distributed jadvalga yozish (asinxron, `distributed_foreground_insert`) vs lokal jadvalga to'g'ridan yozish, distributed so'rov ikki bosqichi (shard'da qisman aggregatsiya, initiator'da yakuniy), `GLOBAL IN`/`GLOBAL JOIN` va ularsiz natija xatosi, `distributed_product_mode`, `parallel_replicas`, qayta sharding qiyinligi, ClickHouse Cloud'dagi SharedMergeTree (compute/storage ajratilgan) bilan farq
20. **Profiling, monitoring va tuning**: `EXPLAIN` turlari (`PLAN`, `PIPELINE`, `indexes = 1`, `ESTIMATE`, `SYNTAX`, `QUERY TREE`), `system.query_log` (`read_rows`, `read_bytes`, `memory_usage`, `query_duration_ms`, `ProfileEvents`) bilan sekin so'rovlarni topish, `system.processes`, `KILL QUERY`, `system.trace_log` va sampling profiler, `send_logs_level = 'trace'`, resurs cheklovlari (`max_memory_usage`, `max_threads`, `max_execution_time`, `max_rows_to_read`, quotas, settings profiles, workload scheduling), concurrency (ko'p kichik so'rov muammosi), backup (`BACKUP TABLE ... TO Disk/S3`, `RESTORE`, `clickhouse-backup`), monitoring (Prometheus endpoint, Grafana ClickHouse plugin), RBAC (`CREATE USER`, `ROLE`, `ROW POLICY`, `GRANT`), UDF: SQL UDF (`CREATE FUNCTION f AS (x) -> ...`) va executable UDF (tashqi skript), ClickHouse Cloud qisqa (SharedMergeTree, avtomatik scaling, narx modeli)

## VI bosqich: Loyiha

21. **Loyiha: Real-time product analytics dashboard**: hodisa generatori (Go yoki Python) Kafka'ga sekundiga minglab hodisa (page_view, click, purchase) yozadi, ClickHouse: Kafka engine + MV + MergeTree (to'g'ri `ORDER BY`, `LowCardinality`, codec'lar), real-time rollup MV'lar (`AggregatingMergeTree` bilan minutlik DAU, funnel, revenue), PostgreSQL'dagi foydalanuvchi profili dictionary orqali boyitish, `ReplacingMergeTree` bilan kech kelgan yoki qayta yuborilgan hodisalarni dedup, TTL bilan xom hodisalarni 30 kunda o'chirib aggregatlarni saqlash, Grafana (yoki oddiy HTML) dashboard: DAU/MAU, funnel konversiyasi, p95 sahifa yuklanish vaqti, cohort retention; `system.query_log` bilan har dashboard so'rovi 500 ms dan tez ekanini isbotlash, bir xil so'rovlarni PostgreSQL va DuckDB'da solishtirish jadvali, README'da sxema qarorlari va "nega"
