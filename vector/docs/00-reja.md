# Vektor bazalar rejasi: embedding, ANN indekslar, pgvector va maxsus vektor DBlar

Nima uchun alohida: LLM va semantik qidiruv davrida "ma'nosi o'xshash narsani top" savoli oddiy `WHERE` bilan yechilmaydi. Matn, rasm, mahsulot embedding (yuzlab yoki minglab o'lchamli son vektori) ga aylantiriladi va eng yaqin qo'shnilar qidiriladi. Millionlab vektor ustida aniq (brute-force) qidiruv sekin, shuning uchun taxminiy eng yaqin qo'shni (ANN) indekslari ishlatiladi: ular recall (aniqlik) ni tezlik va xotira evaziga almashtiradi. Bu modul shu almashuvni tushunish, o'lchash va to'g'ri sozlashni o'rgatadi: avval nazariya va algoritmlar, keyin pgvector (mavjud PostgreSQL ichida, eng ko'p tavsiya etiladigan boshlang'ich nuqta), keyin maxsus vektor bazalar va hybrid qidiruv. `../learn-python/ai/` modulidagi RAG va agent mavzulari shu modulning saqlash qatlamiga tayanadi.

Boshlash: `sql/` I–III bosqich, `postgres/` I va III bosqich (indeks va EXPLAIN tushunchasi). Chiziqli algebra asoslari (vektor, skalyar ko'paytma, norma) kerak; `../learn-python/ml/docs/matematika.md` yetarli. Embedding yaratish uchun Python ishlatiladi (`../learn-python` dagi `.venv` yoki shu loyihadagi `requirements`); lokal model (`sentence-transformers`) yoki tayyor dataset (masalan, ochiq embedding to'plamlari) bilan, pullik API shart emas.

Ishlash tartibi: dars `vector/docs/NN-slug.md`, kod `vector/NN-slug/main.sql` (pgvector) yoki `main.py` (embedding yaratish, Qdrant/Milvus/Chroma klientlari, benchmark), Elasticsearch/OpenSearch kNN uchun `main.sh`, test `test.sql` (pgTAP) yoki `test_main.py`. Ishga tushirish: `make up db=postgres` (pgvector image bilan), `make up db=qdrant` (yoki `milvus`, `weaviate`, `chroma`), `make vec n=04`, testlar `make vec-test n=04`. Dataset va embedding fayllari `vector/data/` da, git'ga kirmaydi.

## I bosqich: Nazariya va algoritmlar
1. **Embedding asoslari**: embedding nima (ma'noni vektor fazoga xaritalash), so'z (word2vec g'oyasi), gap va hujjat (sentence-transformers, bi-encoder), rasm (CLIP, multi-modal), o'lcham (384, 768, 1024, 1536, 3072) va uning xotiraga ta'siri (`dim × 4 bayt × N`), lokal model vs API (Voyage, OpenAI, Cohere) va narx, normalizatsiya, chunking (matnni bo'laklarga bo'lish: o'lcham, overlap, semantik chunking) va nega u qidiruv sifatiga eng kuchli ta'sir qiladi, metadata saqlash, embedding modelini almashtirish = hamma vektorni qayta hisoblash (versiyalash), Matryoshka embedding va o'lchamni qisqartirish, Python'da `sentence-transformers` bilan kichik dataset uchun embedding yaratish va `.npy`/Parquet ga saqlash
2. **Masofa metrikalari va aniq qidiruv**: Evklid (L2), skalyar ko'paytma (inner product), kosinus o'xshashlik va masofa, normalizatsiya qilingan vektorlarda uchalasi bir xil tartib berishi, Manhattan (L1), Hamming va Jaccard (binar vektorlar), model qaysi metrika bilan o'qitilgan bo'lsa shuni ishlatish qoidasi, "o'lchamlar la'nati" (curse of dimensionality) va masofalar konsentratsiyasi, brute-force kNN (NumPy bilan matritsa ko'paytmasi, murakkablik O(N·d)), aniq natija = recall uchun "ground truth", top-k, kichik hajmda brute-force yetarliligi (o'n minglab vektor)
3. **ANN algoritmlari**: nega taxminiy qidiruv (recall vs latency vs xotira vs build vaqti trade-off), daraxt asoslilari (k-d tree, Annoy) va yuqori o'lchamda muammosi, LSH g'oyasi, IVF (inverted file: k-means bilan klasterlash, `nlist`, `nprobe`), Product Quantization (PQ: vektorni subvektorlarga bo'lib kodlash, IVF-PQ, xotirani 10–50x qisqartirish), scalar quantization (float32 → int8) va binary quantization (1 bit, rescoring bilan), HNSW (ko'p qatlamli navigable small world graf, `M`, `ef_construction`, `ef_search`, nega eng mashhur, xotira talabi, o'chirish muammosi), DiskANN/Vamana (SSD'da graf, milliardlab vektor), ScaNN qisqa, FAISS kutubxonasi bilan Python'da IVF va HNSW ni o'lchash, ann-benchmarks.com natijalarini o'qish

## II bosqich: pgvector
4. **pgvector asoslari**: `CREATE EXTENSION vector`, tiplar (`vector(n)`, `halfvec(n)` (float16, yarim xotira), `sparsevec(n)` (sparse, SPLADE kabi), `bit(n)` (binar)), operatorlar (`<->` L2, `<#>` manfiy inner product, `<=>` kosinus masofa, `<+>` L1, `<~>` Hamming, `<%>` Jaccard), `ORDER BY embedding <=> $1 LIMIT 10` pattern'i, funksiyalar (`l2_distance`, `cosine_distance`, `inner_product`, `vector_dims`, `l2_normalize`, `binary_quantize`, `subvector`), aggregatsiya (`avg(embedding)`), embedding'ni Python'dan yuklash (`COPY` binar, `pgvector-python` adapteri, psycopg), saqlash hajmi va TOAST, aniq qidiruv (indekssiz) `EXPLAIN` bilan
5. **pgvector indekslari**: HNSW (`CREATE INDEX ... USING hnsw (embedding vector_cosine_ops) WITH (m = 16, ef_construction = 64)`, `SET hnsw.ef_search`, build vaqti va `maintenance_work_mem`, parallel build), IVFFlat (`lists` tanlash: qatorlar/1000 qoidasi, `ivfflat.probes`, ma'lumot yuklangandan keyin build qilish majburiyati), operator class'lar (`vector_l2_ops`, `vector_ip_ops`, `vector_cosine_ops`, `halfvec_*`, `bit_hamming_ops`), indeks o'lcham chegaralari (2000 o'lcham `vector` uchun, `halfvec` bilan 4000), expression index bilan kvantizatsiya (`(binary_quantize(embedding)::bit(n))` + rescoring), `CREATE INDEX CONCURRENTLY`, HNSW indeksga yozish narxi va VACUUM, `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` da `Index Scan using ... hnsw`
6. **Filtrlash va iterative scan**: ANN + `WHERE` muammosi (indeks top-k ni beradi, filtr keyin ko'p natijani tashlaydi, natija kam qaytadi), pre-filtering vs post-filtering, pgvector 0.8+ da iterative index scan (`hnsw.iterative_scan = strict_order | relaxed_order`, `hnsw.max_scan_tuples`, `ivfflat.iterative_scan`), selektiv filtr uchun oddiy B-tree indeks va planner tanlovi, partial HNSW indeks (har kategoriya uchun), partitioning (tenant bo'yicha), multi-tenant vektor qidiruv, `LIMIT` bilan tuzoqlar va natija sonini tekshirish
7. **pgvector production va masshtab**: xotira hisoblash (HNSW indeksi `shared_buffers`/RAM'ga sig'ishi kerak), `pg_prewarm`, replikalar bilan o'qishni masshtablash, katta hajmda `halfvec` va binar kvantizatsiya, pgvectorscale (StreamingDiskANN, Timescale) va VectorChord kabi alternativ extension'lar qisqa, Citus bilan sharding, managed variantlar (RDS, Supabase, Neon) da pgvector versiyasi, embedding versiyalash va qayta indekslash (blue-green ustun yoki jadval), qachon pgvector yetadi va qachon maxsus DB kerak (asoslash mezonlari)

## III bosqich: Maxsus vektor bazalar
8. **Qdrant**: arxitektura (Rust, collection, point, vektor + payload, segment'lar, WAL), named vectors (bir point'da bir nechta vektor), payload indekslar va filtrlash HNSW ichida (filterable HNSW), kvantizatsiya (scalar, binary, product) va `rescore`/`oversampling`, sparse vektorlar va hybrid qidiruv (Query API, `prefetch` + `fusion: rrf`), multi-vektor (ColBERT late interaction), snapshot va replikatsiya/sharding, REST va gRPC, `qdrant-client` Python bilan CRUD va qidiruv vazifalari, web UI
9. **Milvus va Weaviate**: Milvus arxitekturasi (storage/compute ajratilgan: proxy, query node, data node, index node, object storage, etcd, message queue; Milvus Lite va standalone), indeks turlari (`HNSW`, `IVF_FLAT`, `IVF_PQ`, `DISKANN`, `GPU_*`, `SCANN`), consistency level'lar, partition va partition key, `pymilvus`; Weaviate (schema/collection, vectorizer modullari, `nearText`/`nearVector`, GraphQL va gRPC API, ichki hybrid qidiruv `alpha` bilan, multi-tenancy, dynamic indeks flat → HNSW), ikkalasini docker compose'da ishga tushirib bir xil vazifani bajarish
10. **Chroma, LanceDB va qidiruv enginelardagi vektor**: Chroma (embedded/klient-server, collection, `where` metadata filtri, prototip va notebook uchun, production chegaralari), LanceDB (Lance columnar format, embedded, object storage ustida, data engineering bilan bog'liqligi), Elasticsearch/OpenSearch kNN (`dense_vector` mapping, `knn` query, `num_candidates`, kvantizatsiya `int8_hnsw`/BBQ (Elasticsearch 8.x/9.x), OpenSearch k-NN plugin: Lucene, faiss, nmslib engine'lari), MongoDB Atlas Vector Search (`$vectorSearch` aggregation bosqichi) va Redis vector qidiruv (`FT.CREATE ... VECTOR HNSW`, Redis 8 da Query Engine ichki, `vector set` tipi) qisqa, `../elasticsearch/`, `../mongodb/`, `../redis/` modullariga havola
11. **Vektor DB tanlash va arxitektura**: tanlash mezonlari (hajm, latency talabi, filtr murakkabligi, hybrid zarurati, tranzaksiya va JOIN, ops yuki, mavjud stack, narx, managed: Pinecone, Zilliz, Qdrant Cloud, Turbopuffer kabi serverless), "vektor DB alohida servis" vs "vektor mavjud bazada" qarori, sinxronizatsiya muammosi (asosiy DB va vektor DB o'rtasida: CDC yoki outbox, `../data-engineering/` ga havola), embedding pipeline (ingestion, chunking, batch embedding, qayta hisoblash), xotira va narx hisoblash jadvali, qaror yozish (ADR) mashqi

## IV bosqich: Qidiruv sifati va loyiha
12. **Hybrid qidiruv va reranking**: leksik (BM25, PG FTS, Elasticsearch) va semantik qidiruvning kuchli va zaif tomonlari (aniq atamalar, kodlar, ismlar vs sinonimlar va ma'no), sparse (SPLADE) vs dense, natijalarni birlashtirish (Reciprocal Rank Fusion formulasi `1/(k + rank)`, vaznli kombinatsiya va normalizatsiya muammosi), PG'da SQL bilan hybrid (FTS CTE + vektor CTE + RRF `FULL OUTER JOIN`), cross-encoder reranker (ikki bosqichli: retrieve top-100, rerank top-10), metadata boost (yangilik, mashhurlik), query expansion qisqa
13. **Sifat va unumdorlikni o'lchash**: recall@k (ANN natijasi vs brute-force ground truth), precision, MRR, nDCG (relevantlik bahosi bilan), test so'rovlar to'plami yaratish (qo'lda belgilangan juftliklar, sintetik so'rovlar), latency p50/p95/p99, QPS, indeks build vaqti va xotira, parametrlarni sozlash egri chizig'i (`ef_search` ga qarab recall va latency grafigi), benchmark tuzoqlari (kesh issiqligi, filtrlar, bir xil hardware), `ann-benchmarks` va `VectorDBBench` metodologiyasi, natijani jadval va grafik bilan hisobot
14. **Loyiha: RAG uchun semantik qidiruv servisi**: hujjatlar to'plami (masalan, shu loyihaning `docs/` darslari yoki ochiq dataset) chunking va embedding pipeline'i (lokal model), pgvector'da saqlash (HNSW, metadata, partial indeks yoki tenant filtri, iterative scan), Qdrant'da xuddi shu (hybrid: sparse + dense, RRF), ikkalasini recall@10 va p95 latency bo'yicha solishtirish, hybrid + reranker, embedding versiyasini almashtirish (blue-green), FastAPI yoki Go servis orqali qidiruv API, `../learn-python/ai/` dagi RAG/agent vazifalariga retrieval qatlami sifatida ulash, README'da o'lchov natijalari va tanlov asoslashi

## Ataylab kiritilmagan
Embedding modellarini o'qitish va fine-tuning (`../learn-python/ml/`, `dl/`), LLM bilan generatsiya va prompt (`../learn-python/ai/`), FAISS'ni GPU'da chuqur, vektor DB'ni noldan yozish (faqat brute-force va IVF ni NumPy'da mashq sifatida), Pinecone kabi pullik managed servislarda real hisob. ## Mezon
Har mavzu: kod xatosiz ishga tushadi (`make vec n=NN`), SQL fayllar `sqlfluff lint` toza, Python fayllar `ruff` va `mypy --strict` toza, testlar o'tadi. Indeks va sozlash vazifalarida recall@k va latency raqamlari brute-force ground truth bilan solishtirilgan va kommentga yozilgan. "Tezroq" yoki "yaxshiroq" degan har xulosa o'lchov bilan.
