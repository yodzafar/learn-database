# Theory: ma'lumotlar bazasi nazariyasi va ichki tuzilishi

Nima uchun alohida: vendor modullarida (`postgres`, `mssql`, `oracle`, `mongodb`, `cassandra` ...) har bir DB "qanday ishlatiladi" degan savolga javob beriladi. Bu modul "nima uchun shunday ishlaydi" degan savolga javob beradi: relational model va algebra, normalizatsiya nazariyasi, tranzaksiya va isolation'ning rasmiy ta'riflari, storage engine (page, B+tree, LSM, WAL, buffer pool), query optimizer, recovery (ARIES) va distributed DB (replication, sharding, consensus, consistency modellari). Bu poydevorsiz `EXPLAIN` chiqishi, `VACUUM`, `deadlock`, Cassandra'dagi tombstone yoki MongoDB'dagi `writeConcern` yodlangan qoida bo'lib qoladi; poydevor bilan esa har bir yangi DB bir xil g'oyalarning boshqa kombinatsiyasi bo'lib ko'rinadi.

Boshlash: `../sql/docs/00-reja.md` I bosqichi (1–6 mavzular: DDL, DML, SELECT, NULL) bilan parallel boshlanadi. I bosqich (relational model) SQL bilan birga, II–III bosqichlar SQL II bosqichidan keyin. IV–VI bosqichlar (storage, indeks, query processing) `postgres` modulining arxitektura bosqichi bilan juda yaxshi ulanadi: bu yerda nazariya, u yerda real implementatsiya. VII bosqich (distributed) uchun `../learn-golang` yoki `../learn-python` dagi `distributed/` rejalarining I–II bosqichlari tanish bo'lsa yaxshi, shart emas. IX bosqich (mini-DB loyihasi) Go yoki Python'da yoziladi, shuning uchun o'sha tilda I–III bosqichlar o'tilgan bo'lishi kerak.

Ishlash tartibi: `theory/docs/NN-nom.md` nazariya va vazifalar. Kod va javoblar `theory/NN-nom/` papkasida: `main.sql` (PostgreSQL'da demo: masalan isolation anomaliyasini ikki sessiyada ko'rsatish, `pageinspect` bilan page ichini ko'rish, `EXPLAIN` bilan join algoritmini majburlash), `answers.md` (nazariy savollarga o'z so'zingiz bilan javob, isbotlar, chizmalar matn ko'rinishida) va kerak bo'lsa `test.sql` (pgTAP). Ishga tushirish: `make up db=postgres`, keyin `make th n=05`. Ikki sessiyali vazifalarda (isolation, lock, deadlock) `make shell db=postgres` ni ikki terminalda ochasiz va qadamlarni `main.sql` dagi kommentlarda `-- T1:` / `-- T2:` deb belgilaysiz. Loyiha (IX bosqich) `theory/3x-mini-db/` papkasida Go (`go.mod`) yoki Python (`pyproject.toml`) loyihasi sifatida, testlari bilan.

## I bosqich: Relational model
1. **Ma'lumotlar bazasi tarixi va modellar**: fayl tizimidan DBMS'ga o'tish sababi (data independence, concurrency, recovery), ierarxik model (IBM IMS) va tarmoq modeli (CODASYL), Codd 1970 "A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks" va uning asosiy g'oyasi (mantiqiy va fizik mustaqillik), System R va Ingres, SQL'ning tug'ilishi (SEQUEL), object-oriented DB'lar va nega muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'ldi, NoSQL harakati (2009), NewSQL (2012+), "What Goes Around Comes Around" (Stonebraker va Hellerstein, 2005 va 2024 davomi) tezislari, ANSI/SPARC uch qatlamli arxitektura (external/conceptual/internal), DBMS komponentlari (query processor, storage manager, transaction manager, buffer manager), Codd'ning 12 qoidasi va nega hech bir DB to'liq bajarmaydi
2. **Relational model rasmiy**: domen, atribut, tuple, relation (to'plam, multiset emas), relation schema va instance, tartibsizlik (qator va ustun tartibi yo'q), kalitlar (superkey, candidate key, primary key, alternate key, foreign key), integrity constraintlar (entity integrity, referential integrity, domain constraint), NULL ning Codd modelidagi o'rni va Date/Darwen tanqidi ("The Third Manifesto", NULL'siz model), SQL relational model'dan qayerda chekinadi (dublikat qatorlar, NULL, ustun tartibi, nomsiz ustunlar), set vs bag semantikasi, closure xossasi (har amal natijasi yana relation)
3. **Relational algebra**: asosiy amallar (selection σ, projection π, union ∪, difference −, Cartesian product ×, rename ρ), hosila amallar (intersection, theta join, natural join, equi-join, semi-join ⋉, anti-join ▷, division ÷ va "barcha X ni sotib olganlar" so'rovi), outer join va algebrani kengaytirish, extended algebra (grouping γ, aggregatsiya, sort τ, duplicate elimination δ), bag algebra, algebraik ekvivalentlik qoidalari (selection pushdown, join commutativity va associativity, projection pushdown) va ular optimizer asosida ekanligi, SQL so'rovini algebra daraxtiga o'girish, `EXPLAIN` daraxtini algebra bilan o'qish
4. **Relational calculus va SQL semantikasi**: tuple relational calculus (TRC), domain relational calculus (DRC), Datalog qisqacha (rekursiya va nega rekursiv CTE kerak bo'ldi), Codd teoremasi (algebra va xavfsiz calculus ekvivalent), relational completeness, xavfsiz (safe) ifoda, kvantorlar (∃, ∀) va SQL'da `EXISTS`/`NOT EXISTS` bilan ∀ ni ifodalash (ikki qavatli `NOT EXISTS`), deklarativ vs protsedural so'rov tili, uch qiymatli mantiq (3VL: TRUE/FALSE/UNKNOWN) va uning rasmiy jadvallari, `NOT IN` + NULL paradoksi rasmiy izohi

## II bosqich: Modellashtirish va normalizatsiya
5. **ER va EER modellashtirish**: entity, entity set, atribut turlari (oddiy/kompozit, bir qiymatli/ko'p qiymatli, hosila), weak entity va identifying relationship, relationship va uning darajasi (unary/binary/ternary), kardinallik (1:1, 1:N, M:N) va participation (total/partial), Chen notatsiyasi, Crow's foot notatsiyasi, UML class diagram, EER: specialization/generalization, disjoint/overlapping, total/partial, category (union type), aggregation, ER dan relational sxemaga o'tkazish algoritmi (har holat uchun qoida), merosni jadvalga xaritalash (single table, class table, concrete table) va trade-off'lari, `dbdiagram.io`/DBML va `pgModeler`/DBeaver ER diagrammasi
6. **Funksional bog'liqlik nazariyasi**: functional dependency (FD) ta'rifi, trivial va non-trivial FD, Armstrong aksiomalari (reflexivity, augmentation, transitivity) va qo'shimcha qoidalar (union, decomposition, pseudotransitivity), attribute closure (X⁺) algoritmi, FD to'plamining closure'i (F⁺), kalitlarni FD'dan topish, minimal cover (canonical cover) algoritmi, FD ekvivalentligi, dekompozitsiya xossalari: lossless-join (chase algoritmi va binary test) va dependency preservation, anomaliyalar (insert/update/delete anomaly) va ularning FD bilan bog'liqligi
7. **Normal formalar**: 1NF (atomarlik va uning bahsli ta'rifi: array/JSON ustun 1NF'ni buzadimi), 2NF (partial dependency), 3NF (transitive dependency) va 3NF synthesis algoritmi (lossless + dependency preserving kafolati), BCNF va BCNF decomposition algoritmi (dependency preservation yo'qolishi mumkin), 3NF vs BCNF tanlov, multivalued dependency (MVD) va 4NF, join dependency va 5NF (PJNF), DKNF va 6NF (temporal ma'lumot va anchor modeling bilan bog'liq), har formani buzuvchi va tuzatilgan misol, normalizatsiya amaliyotda qayergacha (odatda 3NF/BCNF)
8. **Denormalizatsiya va amaliy sxema dizayni**: qachon denormalizatsiya (o'qish tezligi, aggregatlar, OLAP), hisoblangan ustun va trigger/materialized view bilan sinxronlash, surrogate vs natural key bahsi (UUID v4/v7, bigint identity, kompozit natural key), EAV anti-pattern va uning o'rniga JSON ustun, polymorphic association, soft delete va uning narxi, temporal ma'lumot (valid time va transaction time, bitemporal model, SQL:2011 period), audit jadval dizayni, multi-tenant sxema strategiyalari (shared table + `tenant_id`, schema per tenant, DB per tenant), dimensional model'ga ko'prik (chuqur: `../data-engineering/docs/00-reja.md` modellashtirish bosqichi)

## III bosqich: Tranzaksiya va concurrency control
9. **Tranzaksiya va ACID**: tranzaksiya ta'rifi va holatlar diagrammasi (active, partially committed, committed, failed, aborted), atomicity (undo), consistency (ilova invariantlari, DB faqat constraintlarni kafolatlaydi), isolation (serializability), durability (WAL va fsync), "ACID marketing" muammosi (har vendor boshqacha tushunadi), BASE qarama-qarshiligi, schedule (serial, serializable), conflict (read-write, write-read, write-write), conflict serializability va precedence graph (sikl testi), view serializability, recoverable, cascadeless va strict schedule'lar, autocommit va implicit tranzaksiya
10. **Isolation darajalari va anomaliyalar**: ANSI SQL-92 to'rt darajasi (Read Uncommitted, Read Committed, Repeatable Read, Serializable) va uchta "phenomena" (P1 dirty read, P2 non-repeatable read, P3 phantom), Berenson va boshq. 1995 "A Critique of ANSI SQL Isolation Levels" (P0 dirty write, P4 lost update, read skew A5A, write skew A5B, Snapshot Isolation darajasi va u ANSI jadvaliga sig'masligi), Adya 2000 (generalized isolation, G0, G1a/b/c, G2-item, G2 dependency graph orqali), Hermitage loyihasi (har DB'da qaysi anomaliya bor), har anomaliyani PostgreSQL'da ikki sessiyada qayta hosil qilish, Oracle "Serializable" aslida SI ekanligi, MySQL Repeatable Read'ning o'ziga xosligi
11. **Lock-based concurrency control**: lock turlari (shared S, exclusive X), lock compatibility matrix, two-phase locking (2PL: growing va shrinking faza), strict 2PL va rigorous 2PL (nega amalda strict), lock granularity va multiple granularity locking (intention lock IS/IX/SIX, ierarxiya: DB, jadval, page, qator), lock escalation (SQL Server), predicate lock va phantom muammosi, gap lock va next-key lock (InnoDB), index-range lock, lock manager ichki tuzilishi (hash table, lock queue), latch vs lock farqi, `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` / `FOR SHARE` / `NOWAIT` / `SKIP LOCKED` va ularning nazariy asosi
12. **Deadlock**: deadlock shartlari (Coffman), wait-for graph va sikl aniqlash, deadlock detection (PostgreSQL `deadlock_timeout`, SQL Server deadlock monitor va victim tanlash), deadlock prevention (wait-die, wound-wait, timestamp asosida), timeout asosidagi yondashuv, lock ordering (ilova darajasida oldini olish), livelock va starvation, PostgreSQL'da deadlock'ni ikki sessiyada hosil qilish va log'ni o'qish, `pg_locks` va `pg_blocking_pids()`, ilovada deadlock'dan keyin retry pattern
13. **MVCC va Snapshot Isolation**: multi-version g'oyasi (o'quvchi yozuvchini bloklamaydi), version chain (append-only, newest-to-oldest, oldest-to-newest), version storage (PostgreSQL: heap'da to'liq tuple, MySQL/Oracle: undo'da delta), snapshot nima (xmin, xmax, active xid list), visibility qoidalari, garbage collection (PostgreSQL VACUUM, InnoDB purge, Oracle undo retention va ORA-01555 "snapshot too old"), first-committer-wins va first-updater-wins, transaction ID wraparound (PostgreSQL 32-bit xid va freeze), "An Empirical Evaluation of In-Memory MVCC" (Wu va boshq. 2017) qisqacha, `xmin`/`xmax`/`ctid` ni PostgreSQL'da ko'rish
14. **Optimistic, timestamp va serializable snapshot**: optimistic concurrency control (OCC: read, validation, write fazalari, Kung va Robinson), basic timestamp ordering (TO) va Thomas write rule, multi-version timestamp ordering (MVTO), Serializable Snapshot Isolation (SSI: Cahill 2008, rw-antidependency, dangerous structure, PostgreSQL implementatsiyasi Ports va Grittner 2012), SSI'da serialization failure va retry, ilova darajasidagi optimistic locking (`version` ustuni, `UPDATE ... WHERE version = $1`), deterministic DB'lar (Calvin) qisqacha, qaysi DB qaysi algoritmni ishlatadi jadvali

## VIII bosqich: DB landshafti va tanlash

33. **DB tanlash metodologiyasi**: ish yuklamasini tavsiflash (o'qish/yozish nisbati, so'rov naqshlari, hajm, o'sish, latency talablari, consistency talablari), "PostgreSQL bilan boshlang" qoidasi va qachon bu noto'g'ri, OLTP/OLAP/HTAP/streaming tanlovi, managed vs self-hosted, litsenziya va narx (Oracle/SQL Server litsenziyalash modellari, SSPL, BSL), operatsion murakkablik, ekotizim va kadrlar, migratsiya narxi, ADR (architecture decision record) yozish, real kompaniyalar keys-stadilari (Discord Cassandra'dan ScyllaDB'ga, Uber Postgres'dan MySQL'ga, Figma Postgres sharding), "boring technology" tamoyili
34. **DB benchmarking va baholash**: TPC-C, TPC-H, TPC-DS, YCSB, `pgbench`, `sysbench`, benchmark tuzoqlari (warm cache, kichik dataset, noto'g'ri percentil, coordinated omission), latency taqsimoti (p50/p95/p99/p999), throughput vs latency egri chizig'i, Little's law, DB-Engines reytingi qanday tuziladi va nimani anglatmaydi, `pgbench` bilan isolation darajalarining throughput'ga ta'sirini o'lchash

## IX bosqich: Loyiha
35. **Loyiha: mini-DB noldan (Go yoki Python)**: bosqichma-bosqich: (1) append-only log KV store (Bitcask modeli, hint file, compaction), (2) disk'da slotted page va buffer pool (Clock replacement), (3) B+tree indeks (insert, search, range scan, split), (4) WAL va crash recovery (redo, checksum, torn write testi), (5) oddiy tranzaksiyalar (strict 2PL yoki MVCC snapshot), (6) kichik SQL qatlami (lexer, parser `SELECT`/`INSERT`/`CREATE TABLE`, AST, volcano iterator executor, filtr va nested loop join), har bosqichda testlar, crash testlar (jarayonni `kill -9` qilib qayta ochish), benchmark va README'da arxitektura chizmasi. Ilhom: CMU 15-445 BusTub, "Build Your Own Database From Scratch" (build-your-own.org), "Database Internals" (Petrov), SQLite arxitekturasi
